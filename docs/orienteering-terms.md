# Orienteering terms in the family's five languages

The apps under controlfreak.se (ControlFreak, ControlFreak Event, ControlFreak Run,
Startklocka, MCP Freak, controlfreak.se) speak Swedish, English, Norwegian (bokmål), Finnish and Czech. Swedish is
the source language: every text is written in Swedish first and translated from it.

This list is the shared vocabulary. When a term below is translated differently in an app,
the app is wrong, not the list. Add a term here when a new one is needed, in all five
languages, in the same pull request as the text that needs it.

The terms follow what the national federations and their rules use: Svenska
Orienteringsförbundet (sv), IOF and British Orienteering (en), Norges Orienteringsforbund
(nb), Suomen Suunnistusliitto (fi) and Český svaz orientačních sportů, ČSOS (cs). Czech
terms were checked against ČSOS's rules of orienteering ("Pravidla OB", 2020).

Language codes: `sv`, `en`, `no` (written as bokmål; `lang="nb"`, `hreflang="nb"`,
`nb_NO`), `fi`, `cs`. Language names on their own language: Svenska, English, Norsk,
Suomi, Čeština.

## Names that are never translated

ControlFreak, ControlFreak Event, ControlFreak Run, Startklocka, MCP Freak, Eventor, ORIS, IRMA, SportIdent
(SI), Emit, IOF, IOF XML, ISOM, ISSprOM, OCAD, Open Orienteering Mapper, Purple Pen,
Condes, Hittaut (a Swedish recreational event; stays "Hittaut" in every language), Swish,
OResults, Liveresultat, GoatCounter, Cloudflare.

## Class names

Swedish and Norwegian write classes H21/D21 (Herrar/Damer, Herrer/Damer). English writes
M21/W21. Finnish writes H21/D21 as well (Finnish federation class names follow the Swedish
pattern). Czech writes H21/D21 (hoši/dívky in youth, muži/ženy: H/D is the common form in
ORIS). Sample data in an app uses the convention of its language.

## Terms to double-check with a native speaker

These are the least certain choices above. They are in use until someone who knows the
language better says otherwise; change them here and in the apps in one go.

- fi: `rastimääritteet` (control descriptions) — also written `rastinmääritteet`.
- fi: `pistesuunnistus` (score orienteering); `hajonta` (forking).
- cs: `údolíčko` (reentrant); `farsta` (forking); `rozmístění kontrol` (control hanging);
  `scorelauf` (score orienteering).
- no: `søkk` (reentrant); `vending av kartet` (map flip).
